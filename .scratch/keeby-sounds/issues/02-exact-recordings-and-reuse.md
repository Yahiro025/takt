## Question

Which exact Keeby recordings are publicly available, and what evidence supports reuse? Record source URLs, formats, mappings, and explicit reuse terms where available. Treat a reachable preview as evidence of availability, not permission to redistribute. Identify inaccessible, incomplete, or permission-unclear entries. Do not equate same-name Mechvibes packs with exact Keeby recordings without evidence.

## Comments

2026-10-01: Resolved with availability and reuse status kept separate. The source report records direct website URLs, the official DMG hash, 873 native WAV/MP3 source hashes, and same-path comparison of 198 site WAVs. The Brown website OGG has no same-named DMG file; it was copied into a separate local website-sprite pack using its recorded SHA-256. No equivalence to the 26 native Brown WAVs is claimed. Three contributor entries permit redistribution as part of the Keeby macOS app, ten kbsim profiles are listed under MIT, and no blanket grant was found for every asset or the website catalog.
